We present ContactMimic, a learning framework that tracks explicit part-level binary contact commands alongside keypoint trajectories. Through contact-following rewards and a trajectory augmentation scheme that breaks the correlation between keypoint trajectories and contact labels, the resulting policy decouples contact behavior from keypoint geometry, achieving precise physical contact and contact-controllability in the real world.
